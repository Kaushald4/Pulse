Questions

The short answers.

Do I need an account? +

No. Pulse is a local desktop app: no sign-up, no sync, no telemetry.

Do I need an API key? +

For scoring, briefings and job matching, yes. Point Pulse at Cloudflare, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Reading and saving work without one.

Is there a subscription? +

No. Pulse is open source. Your only cost is your model provider, and you choose which one.

Does it work on Windows or Linux? +

Not yet. macOS is the tested target today, and both other platforms are in progress.

Where does my data live? +

On your machine. Items are stored locally, and keys stay in your own config file.
